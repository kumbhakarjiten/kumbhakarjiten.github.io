Bootstrap Arguments: From an ODE to a Simple PDE

A self-contained introduction to continuity bootstraps: the first-exit principle, a quadratic estimate with explicit smallness constants, and complete applications to a scalar ODE and a periodic semilinear heat equation. Includes local existence, continuation, and a checklist for reusing the method.
