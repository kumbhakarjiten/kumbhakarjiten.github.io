Mathematical analysis of fluid equations, controllability, and critical-space methods.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Mathematics, Czech Academy of Sciences, working with Professor Šárka Nečasová. My research lies at the intersection of partial differential equations, mathematical fluid mechanics, controllability, and harmonic analysis.

I study compressible Navier–Stokes systems, time-periodic fluid problems, controllability and stabilization of PDEs, and well-posedness questions in Besov-type critical spaces.

About me

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from IISER Kolkata, where I worked under the supervision of Dr. Shirshendu Chowdhury on boundary controllability problems for compressible Navier–Stokes systems and related equations.

Since February 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher in Prague. My current work broadens my earlier control-theoretic research toward time-periodic fluid equations and nonlinear problems in critical Besov spaces.
