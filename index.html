PDEs · Fluid Mechanics · Control Theory

Analysis and control of nonlinear PDEs arising in fluid mechanics.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Mathematics, Czech Academy of Sciences. My research focuses on partial differential equations arising in fluid mechanics and control theory.

My current interests include compressible Navier–Stokes equations, time-periodic problems, controllability and stabilization, fluid–structure interaction, and critical-space methods based on harmonic analysis.

Time-Periodic Fluid Equations

Fluid–Structure Interaction

About

Academic background

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from IISER Kolkata, where I worked under the supervision of Dr. Shirshendu Chowdhury on boundary controllability problems for compressible Navier–Stokes systems and related equations.

Since 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher in Prague, broadening my work toward time-periodic fluid equations, fluid–structure interaction, and nonlinear PDE analysis.
