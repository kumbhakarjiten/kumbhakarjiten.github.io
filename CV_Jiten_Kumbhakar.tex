\section*{Research Profile}
I work on partial differential equations arising in fluid mechanics and control theory. My research focuses on compressible Navier--Stokes type systems, controllability and stabilization, time-periodic PDEs, fluid--structure interaction, and harmonic-analysis methods in critical function spaces. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Mathematics of the Czech Academy of Sciences under the mentorship of Professor \v{S}\'arka Ne\v{c}asov\'a.

\section*{Research Interests}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Partial differential equations and mathematical fluid mechanics.
    \item Compressible Navier--Stokes equations; linearized and nonlinear fluid models.
    \item Boundary controllability, null controllability, observability, stabilization, and insensitizing controls.
    \item Time-periodic PDEs, non-autonomous evolution problems, maximal regularity, and Fourier multiplier methods.
    \item Harmonic analysis methods in PDEs; homogeneous, inhomogeneous, and hybrid Besov spaces.
    \item Compressible fluid--structure interaction, weak solutions, energy and entropy methods.
    \item Coupled parabolic--transport and chemotaxis--fluid control problems.
\end{itemize}
